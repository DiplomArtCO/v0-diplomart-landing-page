# DiplomArt Growth Playbook

## Priority audiences

### Instituciones educativas

- Offer: paquetes de graduación con diploma, acta y carpeta porta diploma.
- Decision makers: rectoría, secretaría académica, compras y coordinación de eventos.
- Core message: materiales premium, personalización institucional, seguridad y cumplimiento de fechas.
- Primary CTA: solicitar cotización para la próxima ceremonia.

### Empresas

- Offer: agendas corporativas, kits empresariales y merchandising personalizado.
- Decision makers: marketing, recursos humanos, compras y organizadores de eventos.
- Core message: branding consistente, personalización completa y entrega puntual.
- Primary CTA: solicitar catálogo y cotización.

## Lead tracking fields

Record these fields for every WhatsApp, phone, email, form, referral, or advertising lead:

`date, source, campaign, audience, product, contact, response_minutes, quote_value, stage, close_date, revenue, notes`

Recommended stages:

`new, contacted, qualified, quoted, won, lost, nurture`

## Weekly review

1. Count leads and qualified leads by source and audience.
2. Calculate median response time and quote rate.
3. Compare cost per qualified lead and revenue by channel.
4. Record the reason for every lost opportunity.
5. Shift effort toward qualified opportunities and closed revenue, not clicks alone.

## Outreach guardrails

- Prefer introductions, referrals, associations, and opted-in contacts.
- Do not buy or bulk-import personal email lists.
- Keep a record of marketing consent and honor opt-outs immediately.
- Identify DiplomArt clearly and include a working unsubscribe path in marketing email.
- Use one relevant case study or catalog item per message and one clear call to action.

## Search Ads test

Create separate campaigns for:

- `diplomas personalizados Cali`
- `paquetes de graduación colegios`
- `agendas corporativas personalizadas`
- `merchandising empresarial Cali`

Use phrase/exact intent, a Cali/Valle del Cauca location target, and negative keywords such as `empleo`, `curso`, `plantilla`, `gratis`, and `imprimir`.
